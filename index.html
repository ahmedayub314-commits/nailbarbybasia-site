Our specialty

What is a Russian manicure?

It's a dry manicure done with an e-file, a small electric tool with fine bits. Instead of soaking and clipping, your tech gently lifts and buffs away dead cuticle skin, so polish can sit right at the cuticle line and grow out cleaner.

It takes longer than a quick manicure. That time is the point.

  1. 1

    Prep and shape

    Nails are shaped and the cuticle area is prepped dry, no soaking.

  2. 2

    E-file cuticle work

    Fine bits remove dead skin around the nail for a clean, even line.

  3. 3

    Close-to-cuticle polish

    No-chip or regular polish applied right up to the cuticle.
