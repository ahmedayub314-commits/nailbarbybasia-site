Questions

What's a Russian pedicure?

A pedicure where the cuticle area is cleaned with an e-file instead of soaking and clipping, for a neat, even line around each nail.

How long does a pedicure take?

A Russian pedicure takes about 1 hour. No-chip pedicures take about 1 hour 15 minutes.

Can I get a manicure at the same time?

Yes. Mani + pedi combos start at $100.

Do you do kids' pedicures?

Yes. The kids no-chip pedicure is $55.
