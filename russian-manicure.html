Our specialty

Russian manicure in Orland Park

A dry, e-file manicure that cleans up the cuticle area precisely, so polish sits right at the cuticle line. It's what our clients write about most.

Book a Russian manicure

How it's different

A classic manicure soaks the hands, pushes the cuticle back and clips. A Russian manicure skips the soak. Your tech uses an e-file with fine, rounded bits to lift and buff away dead skin around the nail, working slowly and carefully.

The result is a very clean cuticle line. Polish can go on right next to it, which is why the finish looks neat for longer as your nails grow.

Questions

How long does it take?

Plan on about an hour for a no-chip Russian manicure, and about 2 hours 15 minutes with a no-chip Russian pedicure.

How much is it?

The no-chip Russian manicure is $60. The no-chip Russian pedicure is $70, or $115 for both together.

Can I get it without no-chip polish?

Yes. Book the Russian manicure with regular polish, or with no polish at all.

Is it the same as a gel manicure?

No. Russian refers to how the cuticle is cleaned, with an e-file instead of soaking and clipping. The finish can be no-chip, regular polish or none.
